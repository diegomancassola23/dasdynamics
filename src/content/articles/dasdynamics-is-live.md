I have worked on Dynamics since 2009: AX 2009, AX 2012, then Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations. Along the way I wrote things down: notes on a Blogger blog, articles on LinkedIn, posts scattered in between. Today they have one home: this site.

The name reads "D as Dynamics", and also "D as Diego". The logo says the same thing: a small D inside a larger one.

## What you find here

**Articles.** Longer pieces on what I work on every day: integrations with Azure API Management and Azure Functions, X++, the platform, and AI inside the ERP, from Copilot to MCP servers. The articles I published on LinkedIn are here too, and this is where I keep them up to date.

**The archive.** My old Dynamics AX notes, 46 of them, kept after an editorial pass that retired the reposts and merged the duplicates. I did not want a copy: every note was re-read against today's D365FO and carries a verdict.

- **Valid**: still works today.
- **Changed**: the idea holds, the way to do it is different; a link to Microsoft Learn shows how.
- **Historical**: AX only, kept for whoever still maintains it.
- **To review**: not checked yet against D365FO.

Some old notes grew into new articles: the archive shows these bridges, from the note to what it became.

## How it works from now on

New articles are published here first. On LinkedIn you will find a short summary and the link. If you prefer, there is an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).

Start from the [articles](/articles/) or the [archive](/archive/). And if something is wrong or out of date, tell me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/diego-mancassola-81a9a11b/): that is how a note stays useful.

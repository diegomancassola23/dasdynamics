Today I open dasdynamics.dev, my site on Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations.

I have worked on Dynamics since 2009, from AX 2009 to D365FO. Over the years I wrote down what I learned in many places: a blog, LinkedIn, notes for myself. Now it all has one home.

The name reads "D as Dynamics", and also "D as Diego".

Here you will find new articles on integrations, X++, the platform and AI inside the ERP. The articles I published on LinkedIn are here too, kept up to date. And my old AX notes, each one re-read against today's D365FO, with a verdict on whether it still holds.

From today I publish here first. On LinkedIn you will find a short summary and the link, or you can follow the [RSS feed](/rss.xml).

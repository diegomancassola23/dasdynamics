Next week I will be in Miami Beach for [Convergence 2026](https://convergence365.com), from Tuesday, December 8 to Friday, December 11, at the Fontainebleau Miami Beach.

Convergence is organized by the Dynamics User Group and sponsored by Microsoft. It brings Microsoft product teams and the Dynamics 365 community together for four days of roadmap and technical sessions across Dynamics 365, the Power Platform and AI.

I am going as an attendee. What I will be listening for, from the Finance & Operations side:

- **AI inside the ERP**: agents, Copilot and MCP in D365FO, and what is ready for real projects.
- **The platform**: Finance & Operations in the Power Platform admin center, environments and the road away from Lifecycle Services.
- **Integrations**: how the roadmap changes the way we connect F&O to the rest of the landscape.

What I learn there will end up here, as articles.

If you are there too and work on Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations, write to me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/diego-mancassola-81a9a11b/): a coffee between sessions is always the best part of these events.

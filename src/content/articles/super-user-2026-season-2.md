I have been recognised as a **Microsoft Power Platform & Dynamics 365 Community Super User for Season 2, 2026**.

It is a small but meaningful milestone in a journey I started earlier this year: making more of my experience available to the community, and offering active, practical support to the people who ask.

## What the Super User program is

The [Dynamics 365 Community](https://community.dynamics.com/) is Microsoft's forum for Dynamics 365 and Power Platform users. Its help page on [becoming a top community contributor](https://community.dynamics.com/help/?partialurl=become-top-community-contributor-mcc-superuser) describes Super Users as top community contributors who "inspire others through example and encouragement—answering forum questions, leading user groups, and creating and publishing their own helpful content."

According to the same page:

- Super User status is earned by accumulating points for community contributions, within a January–June or July–December period.
- To become eligible, a member must earn at least 100 points in that period, including at least 50 points from verified answers.
- To keep the status, the same point levels must be earned again in each following six-month period.
- Super Users get, among other things, moderator privileges, the ability to mark verified answers, blog authoring, access to private Super User discussion areas and invitations to monthly calls with Microsoft.

The current Super Users are listed on the community [leaderboard](https://community.dynamics.com/leaderboard).
